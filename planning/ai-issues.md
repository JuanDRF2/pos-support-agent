# AI issue log — pos-support-agent

A short log of moments the AI got something wrong and had to be corrected — not every mistake,
just the ones worth remembering: a misunderstanding that cost real rework, a pattern that keeps
recurring, a rule that turned out to be missing from `AGENTS.md`. This is different from
`decisions.md` (which tracks what the project does and why) — this tracks how well the AI is
building it, so a repeated failure becomes a signal to fix the rules, not just the code.

Each entry:

### <date> — <short title>
- **What happened:** the mistake, in plain language.
- **Why:** the misunderstanding or missing context that caused it.
- **Fix:** how it was corrected, and whether `AGENTS.md` was updated to prevent it recurring.
