# Gift Renewal POS

How to gift a subscription renewal to another customer's account from the POS, and how to check or cancel a pending gift.

## Gifting a Renewal

A merchant can renew a subscription on behalf of one customer and apply it to a different customer's account — for example, when a customer wants to gift a renewal term to a friend or family member.

1. From the main POS screen, tap **Customers** and open the profile of the customer whose subscription will be renewed (the recipient).
2. Tap **Subscriptions**, select the subscription, and tap **Gift Renewal**.
3. Search for and select the **gifting customer** — the person who will pay for the renewal.
4. Confirm the renewal term and price shown on screen.
5. Take payment from the gifting customer: card, cash, or digital wallet.
6. Once payment is approved, the renewal is applied immediately to the recipient's subscription, and two confirmation receipts are generated: one for the gifting customer (showing the payment) and one for the recipient (showing the extended subscription, with no price shown).

A gifted renewal extends the recipient's subscription by exactly one term from its current expiration date, the same as a regular renewal.

## Checking a Gifted Renewal's Status

1. Open the recipient's customer profile and tap **Subscriptions**.
2. The subscription's **History** tab shows the renewal entry marked **Gifted**, along with the date it was applied. The gifting customer's name is visible to staff but is never shown to the recipient.

## Undoing a Gifted Renewal

A gift renewal is applied immediately and cannot be reversed from the POS once payment is confirmed. If a gift was applied in error, the merchant must process a **standard refund** on the gifting customer's order (see POS Checkout) within the normal 30-day refund window; the recipient's extended subscription term is not automatically reversed and must be adjusted manually by contacting general support.
