# POS Checkout

How to take a payment, issue refunds, and manage receipts at checkout.

## Taking a Payment

1. From the main POS screen, tap **New Sale**.
2. Add items to the cart by scanning a barcode or searching by name.
3. Tap **Charge** and select a payment method: card, cash, or digital wallet.
4. For card payments, the customer taps, inserts, or swipes on the connected card reader.
5. Once the payment is approved, the screen shows **Payment Successful** and offers to email, text, or print the receipt.

A completed sale appears in the **Order History** list within a few seconds, tagged with its order number.

## Issuing a Full Refund

1. Open **Order History** and find the order by order number, customer name, or date.
2. Tap the order, then tap **Refund**.
3. Confirm the full amount and tap **Confirm Refund**.
4. The refund is issued to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear on the customer's statement within 5-7 business days; cash refunds are returned immediately from the drawer.
5. The order status changes to **Refunded** and a refund receipt is generated automatically.

Full refunds are only available within 30 days of the original purchase date. After 30 days, the order can no longer be refunded from the POS and must be handled through general support.

## Issuing a Partial Refund

Use a partial refund when only some items or a portion of the amount should be returned (for example, one item out of a multi-item order).

1. Open **Order History** and select the order.
2. Tap **Refund**, then tap **Partial** instead of confirming the full amount.
3. Either select the specific line items to refund, or enter a custom refund amount (the amount cannot exceed the order total).
4. Review the partial refund summary — it shows the amount being refunded and the remaining order balance.
5. Tap **Confirm Partial Refund**.
6. The refunded amount returns to the original payment method within 5-7 business days for card payments; the order status changes to **Partially Refunded** and a partial-refund receipt is generated showing both the refunded amount and the remaining balance.

Partial refunds follow the same 30-day window as full refunds, measured from the original purchase date.

## Reprinting or Resending a Receipt

1. Open **Order History** and find the order.
2. Tap the order, then tap **Receipt**.
3. Choose **Print**, **Email**, or **Text** to resend it through the original or a new contact method.

Receipts remain available in Order History for 12 months after the purchase date.
