# POS Renewals

How to renew a customer's subscription manually from the POS, retry a failed renewal, and check renewal status.

## Renewing a Subscription Manually

Most subscriptions renew automatically on their billing date, but a merchant can trigger a manual renewal from the POS — for example, when a customer wants to renew early or pay in person.

1. From the main POS screen, tap **Customers** and search for the customer by name, email, or phone number.
2. Open the customer's profile and tap **Subscriptions**.
3. Select the subscription to renew and tap **Renew Now**.
4. Confirm the renewal term and price shown on screen.
5. Select a payment method: card, cash, or digital wallet.
6. Once payment is approved, the subscription's next billing date updates automatically and a confirmation receipt is generated.

A manual renewal does not change the customer's original billing cycle — the next automatic renewal date shifts forward by exactly one term from the subscription's current expiration date, the same rule that applies to gift renewals (see Gift Renewal POS).

## Retrying a Failed Renewal

When an automatic renewal fails (for example, an expired card), the subscription enters a **Past Due** state instead of cancelling immediately.

1. From the main POS screen, tap **Customers** and find the customer.
2. Open **Subscriptions** — a subscription in **Past Due** status is shown with a red label.
3. Tap the subscription, then tap **Retry Renewal**.
4. If the original payment method is still failing, tap **Update Payment Method** first, then retry.
5. Once the retry succeeds, the status returns to **Active** and the next billing date is recalculated from the successful payment date.

A subscription stays in **Past Due** for up to 7 days, during which the merchant or customer can retry payment. If no successful retry happens within that 7-day window, the subscription automatically moves to **Cancelled** status.

## Checking Renewal Status and History

1. From the customer's profile, tap **Subscriptions**.
2. Each subscription shows its current status (**Active**, **Past Due**, or **Cancelled**) and its next or most recent billing date.
3. Tap **History** on any subscription to see a full list of past renewals, including the date, amount, and payment method used for each.

Renewal history is kept for as long as the subscription exists and is not deleted when a subscription is cancelled.
